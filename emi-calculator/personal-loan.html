What is a Personal Loan EMI?

A Personal Loan EMI is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay your personal loan. It includes both principal and interest.

How to Use This Personal Loan EMI Calculator?

Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure to calculate your EMI instantly.

Benefits of Using This Calculator

  • Helps you plan your personal loan
  • Compare different loan options

Tips Before Taking a Personal Loan

  • Check interest rates from multiple lenders
  • Ensure you have a good credit score
  • Choose optimal loan tenure

Personal Loan EMI Example

If you borrow ₹5,00,000 at 12.5% for 36 months, your EMI will be around ₹16,700. Use this calculator to compare alternate tenures and avoid overpaying interest.

When to Choose a Personal Loan

Personal loans are useful for planned expenses, emergencies, or debt consolidation. Compare total repayment cost before selecting your lender.
