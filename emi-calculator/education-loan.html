What is an Education Loan EMI?

An Education Loan EMI is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay your education loan. It includes both principal and interest.

How to Use This Education Loan EMI Calculator?

Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure to calculate your EMI instantly.

  • Compare different loan options

Tips Before Taking an Education Loan

  • Check interest rates and terms
  • Consider government schemes
  • Choose suitable tenure

Education Loan EMI Example

For a ₹10,00,000 loan at 10% for 84 months, EMI is around ₹16,600. Compare tenures to balance affordability and total interest paid.

Moratorium and Repayment Planning

Many education loans offer a moratorium period while studying. Review the lender policy carefully because interest may continue accruing before full EMI repayment starts.
