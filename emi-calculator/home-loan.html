What is a Home Loan EMI?

A Home Loan EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay your housing loan. It includes both principal and interest.

How to Use This Home Loan EMI Calculator?

Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure. The calculator will instantly show your EMI, total interest, and repayment details.
